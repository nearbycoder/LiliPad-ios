LiliPad

A little practice. A lot of possibility.

LiliPad Reading is gentle read-aloud practice for kids on iPad and iPhone. Children read words, sentences and little stories out loud, and LiliPad listens, cheers them on and offers spoken hints.

A little peek at reading, hints, happy hops and growing together. 53 seconds · Original music

Six reading adventures: Word Hop, Sound Safari, Sight Word Stars, Sentence Pond, Sentence Scramble and Story Trail.

Pre-K to 5th grade: each reader's age sets the reading level, and grown-ups can adjust it any time.

Up to six readers, each with their own stars, words and places, plus a name and a buddy emoji or photo.

Private by design: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on the device.
